01 / THE FINAL MESSAGE

The story
ends here.

Living in the Delta has officially come to an end.

Although the project never reached the filming or interview stage, I want to sincerely thank everyone who followed the project, sent messages, shared ideas, engaged with our posts, and showed support along the way.

The project may not have reached the point I originally imagined, but I'm grateful for everyone who showed interest in seeing the stories of the Mississippi Delta documented.
