01 / THE PROJECT

THE DELTA HAS STORIES TO TELL.

We're here
to listen.

Living in the Delta is a documentary project centered on the people, places, history, culture, challenges, and everyday life that shape Mississippi Delta communities.

Season 1 keeps the lens close to home, focusing exclusively on Cleveland and Boyle, Mississippi.

This project is currently in development and is being built through the stories and voices of the communities themselves.
